Why Planora exists

Most productivity tools are good at holding a list. Almost none of them help you decide what to do on a Tuesday afternoon when three things are equally urgent.

Planora started from a simple observation: the hard part of getting things done is rarely remembering what to do. It is the arithmetic — how many hours you actually have, how many hours the work actually takes, and which of those two numbers is lying.

So we built two things and joined them together. First, a task manager that is genuinely pleasant to use on its own: fast entry, honest priorities, a calendar that shows load rather than just dates. Second, a planner that does the arithmetic for you and is willing to say that the week does not fit.

What we believe

  • Refusal is a feature. A planner that promises an impossible schedule is worse than no planner at all.
  • The free tier should be usable. Not a countdown, not a crippled demo. A real task manager you could happily use forever.
  • No theatre. No invented customer counts, no fake testimonials, no made-up discounts. If a number appears on this site, it is a real price.
  • Your data is yours. We do not sell it, we do not advertise against it, and you can delete your account and everything in it.

How the product is put together

Planora runs as a lightweight web application: the marketing site and the planner UI are static and fast, your tasks live with your account, and the AI planning layer turns your description into a schedule through a deterministic scheduling engine. Payments are handled by a subscription provider so card data never touches our servers.
