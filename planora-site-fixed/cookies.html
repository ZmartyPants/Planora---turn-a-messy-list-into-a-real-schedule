Cookie Policy

What Planora stores on your device, and why.

Last updated: 1 January 2025

Policy draft — pending owner review. This document describes how Planora actually handles data in its shipped configuration. Read it, adjust the bracketed details (legal entity, address, provider names, retention periods) to your business, and have it reviewed by a qualified professional before you launch publicly.

1. The short version

Planora sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It stores a small amount of data in your browser so the product works: your session, your preferences, and — in the default local-first configuration — your tasks. None of it is shared with third parties.

2. What we store

NameTypePurposeLifetime
planora:sessionLocal or session storageKeeps you signed in. “Remember me” moves it to local storage; otherwise it ends when the tab closes.30 days / tab
planora:themeLocal storageRemembers light, dark or system appearance.Until cleared
planora:accountsLocal storageCredential record (salted password hash) in local-first mode.Until account deletion
planora:user:*Local storageYour tasks, plans, notifications and preferences in local-first mode.Until account deletion
planora:outboxLocal storageCopy of verification and reset messages in local-first mode so those flows are testable without a mail server.Until cleared

When a hosted backend is configured, task and account data moves to that backend and the corresponding local entries are no longer created.

3. Strictly necessary

Everything listed above is strictly necessary to provide the service you requested — authentication, security and your own settings — so it does not require a consent banner under the ePrivacy Directive.

4. Third parties

Two external resources may be loaded on public pages: Google Fonts (typography) and, if you subscribe to Premium, our payment provider's checkout page (different domain, its own policy). No analytics, advertising or social tracking scripts are used.

To remove the Google Fonts request entirely, self-host the two font families and edit the <link> tags in the page head.

5. Browser notifications

If you enable deadline notifications, your browser issues a permission prompt. That permission is controlled by your browser, not by a cookie, and can be revoked at any time in your browser's site settings.

6. Clearing your data

Settings → Account → Export data gives you a JSON copy of everything. Deleting your account removes it. You can also clear site data in your browser, which will sign you out and, in local-first mode, remove your tasks from that device.

7. Contact

Questions: support@planora.app.
