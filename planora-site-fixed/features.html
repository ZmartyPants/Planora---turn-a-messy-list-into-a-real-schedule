In detail

What each part actually does

Smart task management

Every task carries a title, description, due date, priority, status, estimate, category and free-form notes. Edit anything inline, mark complete with one tap, reopen when plans change.

Priority management

High, medium and low are not decoration: the planner schedules by priority within each deadline, so a high-priority item due Friday lands before a low-priority item due Thursday when time is short.

Due date tracking

Relative labels (Today, Tomorrow, in 3 days), overdue shading and an Upcoming view keep deadlines visible. Tasks without a date are grouped separately rather than silently dropped.

Calendar integration

Month, week and day views. Click any date to create a task there, click a task to edit it. Scheduled blocks from accepted AI plans appear alongside everything else.

Daily and weekly planning

The Today view shows exactly what is on for the next 24 hours. The week view shows load per day so you can see a collision before it happens.

AI task optimisation

Describe a workload in natural language. Planora extracts tasks, resolves “by Wednesday” and “two hours” into structured fields, and shows its reasoning for each inference.

Automatic schedule generation

A deadline-and-priority aware scheduler packs work into your real availability, with 15-minute granularity, existing commitments respected and breaks inserted automatically.

Workload balancing

Daily capacity caps keep one day from absorbing the week. Tasks over three hours are split into sittings across days rather than assigned as one impossible block.

Deadline conflict detection

If work cannot fit before a deadline, Planora says so, names the task, and suggests the specific constraint to change — capacity, working days or the date itself.

Personalised recommendations

Urgent items, overdue debt, fragile estimates and front-loaded days each get a short paragraph explaining what to do about it.
