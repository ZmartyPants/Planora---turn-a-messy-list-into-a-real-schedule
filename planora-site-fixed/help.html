Help Center

Short, practical answers to the things people actually get stuck on.

Getting started

Create an account, confirm your email, then follow the four-step onboarding. It asks for your timezone, working hours, time format and break habits so the planner has realistic defaults from day one. You can skip any step and revisit it from Settings.

Creating your first task

Press “Add task” on the dashboard, or use Quick actions. Only the title is required — everything else can be filled in later. Tasks with a due date appear in Today, Upcoming and the calendar automatically.

Why a task is missing from my calendar

The calendar places tasks by due date. Tasks with no due date live in My Tasks → “No due date” and are not shown on a day. Give them a date and they will appear.

How AI Plans are saved

Nothing is written to your task list until you press Accept. Once accepted, the schedule is saved as a plan and the tasks appear on the calendar and dashboard. Completing a scheduled task updates the plan automatically.

Resetting your password

Use “Forgot password?” on the login page. A single-use reset link is issued and expires after one hour. If it has expired, request another.

Cancelling Premium

Settings → Subscription → Cancel subscription. The next charge stops immediately; Premium continues until the end of the paid period and then the account returns to Free. Tasks are untouched.

Deleting your account

Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, tasks, plans and notifications. It cannot be undone, so export anything you need first.
