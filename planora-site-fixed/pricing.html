Prices in USD. Paid monthly, in advance. No hidden fees, no automatic upsells, no annual lock-in. Payments are handled by our payment provider — Planora never stores your card details.

How billing works

Free. $0 forever. No card required, no expiry, no feature timer.

Premium. $4 USD per month, charged in advance on the same date each month. Your card is handled by our payment provider; Planora never receives or stores card numbers.

Cancelling. Settings → Subscription → Cancel. The cancellation stops the next charge immediately, and Premium remains active until the end of the period you have already paid for. After that your account drops to Free.

If a payment fails. We retry through the provider and tell you in-app. Premium access pauses until a payment succeeds; nothing is deleted.

Your data. Cancelling or downgrading never removes tasks, notes or saved plans. If you delete your account deliberately, everything associated with it is removed.

VAT/sales tax may be added at checkout depending on your billing country, as required by law.
