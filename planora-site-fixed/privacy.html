Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and how to get rid of it.

Last updated: 1 January 2025

Policy draft — pending owner review. This document describes how Planora actually handles data in its shipped configuration. Read it, adjust the bracketed details (legal entity, address, provider names, retention periods) to your business, and have it reviewed by a qualified professional before you launch publicly.

1. Who we are

Planora (“we”, “us”) operates the Planora web application and website. For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR, we are the controller of the personal data described below. Our contact address for privacy matters is support@planora.app and our postal address is [company name and registered address to be completed].

2. What we collect

CategoryExamplesWhy
Account dataName, email address, password hash, email-verification status, account creation dateTo create your account, authenticate you and secure it
Task contentTitles, descriptions, due dates, priorities, categories, notes, estimates, completion statusTo provide the core product
Planning dataSaved AI plans, scheduled blocks, your working hours, timezone, break preferencesTo generate and store schedules

We do not collect advertising identifiers, and we do not sell personal data.

3. Legal bases

  • Contract — processing your account and task data is necessary to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — security monitoring, fraud prevention, debugging and improving the product.
  • Consent — optional browser notifications. You can withdraw at any time in Settings → Notifications or in your browser.
  • Legal obligation — invoicing, tax and accounting records for paid subscriptions.

4. Where your data lives

Task and account data is stored in the database associated with your Planora deployment. If you use Planora hosted by us, data is stored in the EU/EEA region of our infrastructure provider. If you self-host Planora, your data never leaves your own infrastructure.

In the shipped default configuration the application stores your data in your own browser's local storage on your device; nothing is transmitted to us. When a hosted backend is configured (see the deployment guide), the same data is synchronised to that backend so you can sign in from other devices.

5. Who else is involved

We use a small number of processors, each under a data-processing agreement:

  • Payment provider (e.g. Stripe) — subscription billing and invoices.
  • Email provider (e.g. a transactional email service) — verification and password-reset messages only.
  • Hosting and database provider — serving the application and storing data.
  • AI provider — only if an AI endpoint is configured, the text you submit to the AI Planner is sent to that provider to extract tasks and draft a schedule. It is not used to train their models under our configuration.

We do not share task content with advertisers, data brokers or any other third party.

6. How long we keep it

Account and task data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your tasks, plans, notifications and preferences are deleted immediately and irreversibly. Billing records are retained for the period required by tax law (typically seven years). Support messages are deleted after 24 months.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, object to processing of, and delete your personal data, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Most of these are available inside the product: edit your profile in Settings, export your data to a JSON file in Settings → Account, and delete your account there too. For anything else, email support@planora.app — we respond within 30 days.

8. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes. Sessions are per-device tokens that you can end by signing out. Access to task data is scoped to the account that owns it. Transport is encrypted with TLS. We deliberately keep a small attack surface: no third-party advertising scripts, no tracking pixels on private pages.

9. Children

Planora is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes

If we make a material change to this policy we will notify you in-app and update the date at the top of this page.
